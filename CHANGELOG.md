# Changelog

What changed in each release, newest first. A release is described here
before `scripts/release.sh` is run; the same text becomes its notes on GitHub.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- **Summary**: a dashboard of CPU, power, temperature, GPU, memory, network, disk, fans, the Neural Engine and overall system pressure, with the busiest processes. Click the CPU chart to look back in time; click the pressure tile to see what it is made of.
- **Performance**: a page each for CPU, memory, disk, network, GPU, NPU, energy and thermals.
- **Processes**: applications and background processes in two groups, flat or as a tree, with a steady order, highlights where numbers change, columns you choose from the header menu, details of the selected process with its path and command to copy, and Quit and Force Quit.
- **Sensors**, **Apple Silicon** (per-core charts and every media engine block), **Connections**, **System Info**, **Startup apps**, **Users**, **Services**, **Installed Apps** and **Drivers**.
- **Disk Space**: scan a folder and see what fills it, as a list or a bubble map, and move things to the Trash.
- **Menu bar**: CPU, GPU and memory percentages and network speed, each with its own switch in Settings.
- **App icon**: a Control key with a level meter.
- **Privileged helper** (optional): shows CPU and memory of system processes and lets you quit them.
